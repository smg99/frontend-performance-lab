# Product Positioning

## Category

Frontend Performance Lab (FPL) is an AI-Assisted Deterministic Performance Engineering Platform.

FPL is not simply another linting tool.

FPL is not another Lighthouse replacement.

FPL is not another AI chatbot.

FPL creates a new category where deterministic engineering and Artificial Intelligence work together.

Deterministic analysis discovers facts.

Artificial Intelligence explains those facts.

---

# The Problem

Modern AI assistants are excellent at generating code.

They are significantly weaker at proving engineering correctness.

Performance engineering remains difficult because:

- performance problems are often invisible
- regressions are discovered too late
- developers rely on intuition
- recommendations are inconsistent
- explanations lack evidence

Developers deserve better.

---

# Our Solution

Frontend Performance Lab continuously analyzes frontend applications using deterministic techniques.

Instead of asking developers to become performance experts, FPL brings expert guidance directly into their IDE.

Every recommendation is:

- deterministic whenever possible
- explainable
- actionable
- educational
- reproducible

---

- Technical Leads
- Performance Specialists

Secondary audiences include:

- Open Source Maintainers
- Engineering Managers
- Technical Trainers

# Who FPL Is NOT For

FPL is not intended to:

- replace code review
- generate entire applications
- become another generic AI assistant
- compete with IDEs
- compete with frameworks
- replace browser developer tools

Those products already exist.

FPL complements them.

---

# Why Developers Will Choose FPL

Developers choose FPL because it provides something AI alone cannot:

Deterministic engineering confidence.

Developers should feel comfortable acting on FPL recommendations because every recommendation is backed by evidence rather than probability.
