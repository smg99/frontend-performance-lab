# The Frontend Performance Lab Manifesto

> We believe performance is a feature.
>
> We believe trust is earned.
>
> We believe engineering deserves evidence.

---

## Why We Exist

Modern software development is changing faster than ever.

Artificial Intelligence can generate thousands of lines of code in seconds.

Yet generating code is not the same as engineering software.

Performance problems remain some of the most difficult issues to identify, explain, and fix.

Many of these problems cannot be solved by intuition alone.

They require deterministic analysis.

Frontend Performance Lab exists to bring deterministic engineering back into an AI-first world.

---

## What We Believe

We believe developers deserve tools they can trust.

A tool should never invent problems simply because an AI model believes something "might" be wrong.

Every important recommendation should have evidence.

Every warning should be explainable.

Every report should teach something useful.

Trust is more valuable than the number of warnings generated.

---

## AI Is Our Partner

Artificial Intelligence is one of the greatest productivity improvements software engineering has ever seen.

We embrace it.

But AI should remain an engineer's assistant—not the engineer.

FPL uses AI to explain findings, educate developers, prioritize work, and improve communication.

FPL never asks AI to replace deterministic analysis.

Facts come first.

AI comes second.

---

## Local First

Your source code belongs to you.

Performance analysis should happen where your code lives.

On your machine.

Inside your editor.

Without requiring your repository to be uploaded to a cloud service.

Privacy is not a premium feature.

Privacy is a principle.

---

## Education Over Noise

Developers should become better engineers after using FPL.

Every report should answer:

- What happened?
- Why does it matter?
- How can I verify it?
- How should I fix it?
- Why is this solution better?

If we cannot explain a recommendation clearly, we should reconsider making it.

---

## Open Engineering

Great engineering tools grow through communities.

We want contributors to build analyzers.

Diagnostics.

Recipes.

Plugins.

Reports.

IDE integrations.

Educational content.

Without needing permission to reinvent the core.

The ecosystem should become stronger than the product itself.

---

## Engineering Over Hype

We refuse to optimize for GitHub stars.

We refuse to chase trends.

We refuse to build features simply because competitors have them.

We optimize for engineering quality.

Developer trust.

Long-term maintainability.

Everything else follows.

---

## The Future

We imagine a future where every frontend developer has access to world-class performance engineering knowledge.

Not because they spent years specializing.

But because the right tools quietly guide them while they work.

That future begins with deterministic analysis.

Everything else builds on top of it.

---

## Our Promise

We will never knowingly trade developer trust for short-term growth.

We will never sacrifice correctness for impressive demos.

We will never stop learning from real-world engineering.

Frontend Performance Lab will always exist to help developers build software that is faster, simpler, and more reliable.

One repository.

One component.

One engineer at a time.
