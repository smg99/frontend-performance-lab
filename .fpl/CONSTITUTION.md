# Frontend Performance Lab Constitution

Version: 1.0

Status: Active

This Constitution defines the permanent principles governing Frontend Performance Lab.

Mission, Vision, roadmaps, plugins, releases, engineering decisions, and future business models must comply with this Constitution.

If any document conflicts with this Constitution, the Constitution takes precedence.

---

# Article I — Purpose

Frontend Performance Lab exists to help developers build faster, simpler, and more reliable frontend applications through deterministic engineering.

Artificial Intelligence is an accelerator.

Deterministic analysis is the foundation.

---

# Article II — Local First

Developer source code belongs to the developer.

FPL shall always prioritize local execution.

Cloud services may enhance the experience but shall never become mandatory for core functionality.

Privacy is a permanent product principle.

---

# Article III — Deterministic First

Whenever deterministic analysis can prove an engineering conclusion, that conclusion becomes the source of truth.

Artificial Intelligence may:

- explain
- summarize
- prioritize
- educate
- recommend

Artificial Intelligence shall never replace deterministic findings.

Heuristic suggestions must always be clearly distinguishable from proven findings.

---

# Article IV — Trust

Developer trust is the highest priority.

Every release must increase trust.

False positives are considered more damaging than missed opportunities.

Every warning should be explainable.

Every recommendation should be reproducible.

Every report should be evidence-based.

---

# Article V — Evidence

The product evolves through evidence.

Evidence includes:

- dogfooding
- production repositories
- benchmarks
- false positives
- false negatives
- community feedback

No feature shall be implemented solely because a competitor offers it.

---

# Article VI — Education

FPL is an engineering mentor.

Every finding should answer:

What happened?

Why does it matter?

How can it be verified?

How should it be fixed?

Why is the recommendation better?

Every interaction should leave the developer with greater understanding.

---

# Article VII — Platform Thinking

Frontend Performance Lab is a platform.

The platform consists of independent layers.

Each layer must remain independently testable.

Each layer must expose stable interfaces.

Future plugins should extend existing layers instead of modifying them.

---

# Article VIII — Extensibility

Every major subsystem should be designed for extension.

Plugins should be preferred over modifications.

Public APIs should remain stable.

Breaking changes require documented justification.

---

# Article IX — Simplicity

Complexity requires justification.

The simplest maintainable solution should always be preferred.

Abstractions should exist only when they clearly reduce long-term maintenance.

---

# Article X — Engineering Quality

Every change must improve one or more of the following:

- correctness
- maintainability
- readability
- performance
- developer experience
- extensibility

If it improves none of them, it should not exist.

---

# Article XI — AI Independence

Frontend Performance Lab belongs to no AI vendor.

FPL shall remain compatible with multiple IDEs, editors, and AI assistants.

Support for one ecosystem must never prevent support for another.

Vendor neutrality is a strategic advantage.

---

# Article XII — Open Source

Engineering decisions should be documented.

Contributors should understand why decisions exist.

The project should encourage learning, experimentation, and respectful collaboration.

Community contributions strengthen the platform.

---

# Article XIII — Long-Term Thinking

Every architectural decision should remain understandable years after it is implemented.

Temporary convenience must never become permanent technical debt.

Long-term maintainability always outweighs short-term speed.

---

# Article XIV — Product Integrity

FPL shall never knowingly sacrifice engineering quality for marketing.

Popularity is not success.

Developer trust is success.

GitHub stars are not success.

Engineering excellence is success.

---

# Article XV — Continuous Improvement

Every repository analyzed...

Every benchmark executed...

Every plugin published...

Every community contribution...

Every engineering discussion...

makes the platform stronger.

Frontend Performance Lab is never considered "finished."

Continuous improvement is part of its identity.

---

# Amendment Process

The Constitution should change rarely.

Amendments require careful consideration because they affect every future engineering decision.

Roadmaps may evolve.

Implementations may evolve.

Technologies may evolve.

The Constitution should remain stable.
